## Conservative defaults (owner unknowns — log each in `implementation-notes.md`)
1. Any active member can create, edit and post invoices; **cancel is whatever the database allows** (read `cancel_document`). The UI shows what the DB will accept, never more.
2. Credit sales over the customer's limit are refused; only an owner can override, and the override is a deliberate second click.
3. Walk-in customer bills are **cash sales by default** (the Post-as-cash-sale button is the primary action when the walk-in party is chosen); credit to walk-in is refused by the DB's zero limit anyway.
4. Invoice date defaults to today; back-dating is allowed within an open period (the DB refuses locked periods); future dates are refused by the form.
5. Rate and discount are editable by any member (the owner has not asked for a price lock) — log it as an owner question.
6. A draft has no invoice number; the number appears only when posted.
7. Only customer/both parties and stock items are selectable; a disabled party or item cannot be added to a new draft (an existing draft keeps its snapshot).
